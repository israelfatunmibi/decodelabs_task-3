# Project 3: The Visual Identity

**UI/UX Design Internship, DecodeLabs (Batch 2026)**

**Author:** Israel Fatunmibi

## Scenario
A premium organic food delivery service wants a modern, "fresh" look. The brand identity needs to communicate trust, health and speed through color and typography.

## Brand: Farmlane
Tagline: *Fresh from the farm to your door.*

## Deliverables
1. **Style tile**: color palette and typography
2. **Lo-fi Wireframes**
3. **Splash screen**
4. **Product list**
5. **Product details**

## Design decisions
- **Color:** a natural palette built to signal health, trust and freshness.
  - **Deep Leaf Green** `#0A631B` (primary): splash background, buttons, active states, links
  - **Sage** `#073F11` at 45% opacity, rendering as `#889C73` (secondary): trust banners and the harvest-detail card
  - **Fresh Leaf** `#127D24` at 45% opacity, rendering as `#8DB87C` (tint): Certified organic and Non-GMO badges
  - **Harvest Yellow** `#EEC729` (accent): logo leaf, loading ring and cart count, used sparingly
  - **Cream** `#F1E8C4` (background): warmer and more natural than stark white
  - **White** `#FFFFFF` (surface) and **Black** `#000000` (text)
  - No urgent reds appear in the interface, to avoid a fast-food or alarm feel
  - Contrast checked against WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum): white on Deep Leaf 7.5:1, black on Cream 17.1:1, black on Harvest Yellow 12.8:1, Deep Leaf on Cream 6.1:1, black on the Sage banner 7.1:1, black on the Fresh Leaf badge 9.3:1
- **Typography:** Inter throughout, in a single family.
  - Chosen for screen legibility, a wide weight range and clear numerals for prices
  - **Weights:** Bold, SemiBold, Medium and Regular, used to build hierarchy without a second typeface
  - **Type scale:**
    - 64 Bold: splash wordmark
    - 30 Bold: product list screen title ("What’s fresh today?")
    - 24 Bold: product name on the details screen
    - 20 Bold: section headings ("Fresh picks") and the price on the details screen
    - 16 Bold: buttons, card prices and card titles
    - 16 Regular: body text, descriptions and search
    - 14 Medium: "Certified organic" labels on product cards
    - 14 Regular: ratings and secondary information
    - 12 Regular: harvest notes, tab labels and stat labels
  - Hierarchy comes from size and weight, and body text is never smaller than 16px except for secondary information (14px) and captions (12px)
- **Trust:** harvest time, farm name and organic badges appear before product details
- **Speed:** a loading state on the splash, a delivery window under the search
- **Spacing:** 8pt grid, 16px margins, 48px+ primary buttons, 44px minimum tap targets
- **Accessibility:** text contrast checked against the 4.5:1 minimum
